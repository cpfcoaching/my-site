## Test your SOC 2 Type II readiness before you start fieldwork

Small SaaS teams often feel SOC 2 pressure before they have a clean readiness system. The expensive mistake is starting with tooling, audit scheduling, or customer promises before the team knows what is in scope, who owns each area, and where evidence lives.

This browser-based assessment helps you answer five practical questions:

- What is in scope?
- Who owns the readiness areas?
- Where does evidence live?
- Which gaps would slow down audit prep or customer review?
- Have your controls operated long enough to produce representative Type II evidence?

Important: this is not an audit, legal advice, compliance proof, or a guarantee of SOC 2 readiness. Confirm scope, criteria, controls, and evidence expectations with your CPA firm.
