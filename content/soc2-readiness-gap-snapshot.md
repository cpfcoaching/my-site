## Find obvious SOC 2 readiness gaps before you spend on audit prep

Small SaaS teams often feel SOC 2 pressure before they have a clean readiness system. The expensive mistake is starting with tooling, audit scheduling, or customer promises before the team knows what is in scope, who owns each area, and where evidence lives.

This limited snapshot helps you answer four practical questions:

- What is in scope?
- Who owns the readiness areas?
- Where does evidence live?
- Which gaps would slow down audit prep or customer review?

Important: this is not an audit checklist, legal advice, compliance proof, or a guarantee of SOC 2 readiness. Use it internally to identify obvious cleanup actions.
